How to Use

  1. In Banner, go to your registration schedule and use the Print view to save your schedule as a PDF.
  2. Drop that PDF above - everything happens locally in your browser.
  3. Check the preview table, then Download the .ics file
  4. In Google Calendar: Settings → Import & export → Import, and select the downloaded file. (Works in Apple Calendar and Outlook too.)
